Frequently Asked Questions

+

+

What is HOSA?

+

HOSA-Future Health Professionals is an international student organization recognized by the U.S. Department of Education and the Health Science Technology Student Association.

+
+
+

Who can join?

+

Any student enrolled in a Health Science Education program is eligible to join HOSA.

+
+
+

How do I register for events?

+

Use the Registration tab above to sign up for competitive events. Make sure to select your preferred event and submission type.
